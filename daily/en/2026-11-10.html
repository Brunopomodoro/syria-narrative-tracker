Officials from both sides described bilateral ties as strong despite the recent tension.

Reports also mention a possible Syrian move to join the Mecca Joint Defense Agreement with Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan. Officials call the cooperation training-focused, not combat deployment.

Separately, the Amjad Youssef case was referred to the Public Prosecutor, with Justice Minister Mazhar al-Weis meeting US rule-of-law officials in Washington.

US envoy Tom Barrack praised the move as historic, while commentators and lawyers called it a step toward judicial accountability.
  7. Syria-Lebanon Border Crossings Reopen After Years of Closure International relations · People: leaning positive · Outlets: strongly positive · 2% of the discussion

Lebanese officials welcomed the reopening, while Syrian customs officials said the Arida crossing would open next month, alongside broader coordination with Lebanon on border security and refugee return.

1. The day at a glance

Divided Discussion this period centered on reports that Syria is sending troops or advisers to Saudi Arabia amid the Houthi-Saudi escalation, drawing sharp public debate between support for Gulf solidarity and anger that Syria would fight abroad while Israel occupies Syrian territory. The Amjad Youssef transitional justice case, continuing Israeli incursions in Quneitra, a deadly family shooting in Aleppo's Amiriya, and new governance decrees also drew heavy engagement. Outlets largely stuck to official and procedural framing, while public comments were more emotional, skeptical and often religious or sarcastic in tone.

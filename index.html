The move triggered heated debate over free expression versus moral and economic priorities.
  • Syria Recovers Ilyushin Aircraft Stranded in Libya Since Assad's Fall Syria's Defense and Foreign Ministries announced the recovery of an Ilyushin military transport aircraft that had been stranded at Benina airport in Benghazi, Libya, since December 2024, days before Assad's fall. Officials thanked Libyan authorities, including General Khalifa Haftar, for cooperation. Commenters speculated the plane had ferried fleeing regime officers and looted equipment to Libya.

The Syrian Observatory documented 117 Israeli strikes on Syria since early 2026. Local outlets and OSINT trackers continue cataloguing near-daily incidents.

A Syria Partnership Coordination Forum is set to launch in Damascus on 17 November, co-chaired by Foreign Minister Asaad al-Shaibani and EU Commissioner Dubravka Suica.
  • Fatal Fuel Tanker Collision and Fire on Homs-Tartus Road A collision between two fuel tankers near the Haythamiya descent on the Homs-Tartus road on 12 November sparked a fire, killing at least three people according to Civil Defence. The road was temporarily closed and diverted via the old Dabbusiya route while crews extinguished the blaze.
  • Voluntary Return Convoys Continue from Hasakah Camps to Original Towns Hundreds of displaced families returned from Washokani and other camps in Hasakah countryside to Ras al-Ain after roughly seven years, following mine clearance and transport provided by authorities. The eighth voluntary return convoy moved 373 families; officials met UNHCR to discuss sustaining the process.
  • Raqqa Protests Over Fuel Exports Amid Local Shortages Protesters in Raqqa set tires ablaze and blocked roads to prevent oil tankers from reaching Damascus and Turkey, denouncing fuel exports while residents suffer diesel and heating fuel shortages locally.
  • Armed Clash Between Families in Alma, Daraa Recurs A week-long armed clash between two families in Alma village, eastern Daraa countryside, recurred on 12 November, wounding six people including one critically. Security forces intervened to calm the situation after earlier flare-ups left others injured.

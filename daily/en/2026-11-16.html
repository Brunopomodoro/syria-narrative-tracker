Israeli incursions into Quneitra and Daraa continued, with SANA reporting raids and home searches in Maariyah village, and Syria Direct, an independent outlet, describing Palestinian families' fears of a 'second Nakba' amid Israel's expanding presence in the south. Transitional justice proceedings also featured, with Al-Watan Online reporting trials in absentia of Bashar al-Assad, Ali Mamlouk and others over crimes in Deir ez-Zor, and SANA detailing a Damascus court session.

Economy

Economic coverage centred on the Syrian pound's continued slide, which The Syrian Observer attributed to trade deficits, weak production and fiscal strain, while Syria TV, an independent Syrian outlet, examined the budget deficit and rising imports as drivers. Multiple outlets, including SANA and Enab Baladi, an independent outlet, reported Syria's central bank approved a new Islamic bank with $150 million in capital through a Jordanian partnership. Saudi-Syrian cooperation featured prominently, with SANA and Zaman al-Wasl reporting a deal to explore oil shale deposits in Aleppo province, and Syria TV noting talks on opening a land crossing through Iraq. Enab Baladi and SANA reported the government set purchase prices for corn and cotton for the 2026 season, while Syria TV reported power outages have returned to burden residents of Damascus countryside. Enab Baladi examined marketing hurdles facing Suwayda's saffron crop, and Enab Baladi noted unclear tourism revenue figures despite responsive markets.

Governance

EU High Representative Kaja Kallas's visit to Damascus dominated governance coverage, with SANA, The Syrian Observer and Turkish state-run Anadolu Agency all reporting the bloc's intent to elevate ties and move forward with a partnership agreement. Syria TV and Al-Watan Online quoted Kallas saying Syria needs major investments to build institutions, while Hawar News, affiliated with the Kurdish-led administration, and ANHA also covered the EU engagement. UN officials told the Security Council that progress is 'real but fragile', per UN News, and that Syrians 'cannot afford more instability', per OCHA. UNICEF and a joint Ministry of Health-WHO-UNICEF statement via The Syria Report reported a polio vaccination campaign launched in Deir-ez-Zor. Middle East Forum, a think tank often critical of the Kurdish-led administration and generally pro-Israel in orientation, analysed power dynamics in north-east Syria, while Syrian Observatory for Human Rights reported Hama landowners protesting land expropriation.

Divided Discussion is dominated by speculation over possible Syrian troop deployment to Yemen alongside Saudi Arabia, which Damascus repeatedly denies, drawing mockery and concern that Israeli incursions in the south are being ignored. Outlets highlight security crackdowns on former regime officials, Kurdish returns to Ras al-Ain, and diplomatic meetings with the US envoy, while public commentary on Telegram, X and YouTube is skeptical of official statements and focused on Israeli incursions, the Suwayda clashes, and the missing Alfa Watan sailors. Overall mood is wary, with sarcasm toward both Gulf and Syrian officials and persistent anger over Israeli actions in the south.

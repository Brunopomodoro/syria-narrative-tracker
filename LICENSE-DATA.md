# Data licence

The data produced by the Syria Narrative Tracker (everything under `data/`, the story pages under `stories/`, the weekly digests under `weekly/`, and the text shown on the website) is published under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)**.

> Syria Narrative Tracker, https://www.syrianpulse.org/, CC BY 4.0 (retrieved DATE)

Full licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Please note:

- The analyses are generated automatically by a language model from public posts. They describe what is being said online, not what is true. Cite the retrieval date, because the analysis is updated continuously.
- Outlet headlines in the data are short excerpts of public news items with links to their sources; the rights in the underlying articles remain with their publishers.
- No posts by individuals are included, and none should be reconstructed from the data.

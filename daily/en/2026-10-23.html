    Israeli forces killed Mouaz Salama al-Ba'ir, a young man with a disability, during an incursion in western Daraa countryside, triggering protests across Syrian governorates on Friday, 23 October. Separately, Israeli forces briefly detained and later released a shepherd near Hadar in Quneitra, and shelled areas near Hamidiya and Zubaida amid a fire in Jabata al-Khashab.

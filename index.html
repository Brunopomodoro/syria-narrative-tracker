Anxious Coverage and public discussion this period center heavily on Israeli military activity in southern Syria, especially the expanding 'Sufa 53' line in Quneitra and repeated incursions/killings, which outlets across the spectrum condemn while ordinary Syrians express anger, fatalism and mockery of official rhetoric. A major Eagle Hills investment agreement signed by the presidency drew mixed public reaction between hope for jobs and cynicism about reconstruction priorities. Kurdish-language and north-east issues remain prominent, including Hasakah flag-burning controversy, Kurdish education rights, and reports of thousands transferred to Iraq facing possible execution. Economic hardship, heating costs, and viral moments like the walking minister continued to generate everyday public commentary, often skeptical of government messaging.

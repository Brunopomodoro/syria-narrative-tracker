The day's stories

  1. Jordanian airstrikes on Daraa and Suwayda kill civilians, spark diplomatic crisis Israel and the south · People: strongly negative · Outlets: leaning negative · 78% of the discussion
    Jordan struck alleged ISIS sites in southern Syria on 26-27 October, killing at least seven to eight civilians including women and children, in declared retaliation for a church attack in al-Karak. Damascus denied prior coordination, closed then reopened the Nasib crossing, and the two foreign ministers agreed to form a joint security committee. Funerals were held in Daraa as officials from both countries traded explanations.
  2. President al-Sharaa's extensive interview on Syria-Iraq relations ahead of PM visit International relations · People: mostly neutral · Outlets: leaning positive · 11% of the discussion
    President Ahmad al-Sharaa gave an extended interview to Iraq's Dijlah TV covering detainees, ISIS risks, oil and water cooperation, and historic ties, ahead of Iraqi PM Ali al-Zaidi's visit to Damascus on 28 October. He said over 3,000 Syrians held by Iraq should be repatriated and praised al-Zaidi's anti-corruption drive.
  3. Attacks on Kurdish families returning to Tal Abyad continue North-east and the Kurdish question · People: strongly negative · Outlets: leaning negative · 2% of the discussion
    Reports and videos circulated showing vehicles of displaced Kurdish families burned in Tal Abyad (Girê Spî) on 26 October, prompting protests across north-east Syria. Kurdish-language social media accuses Arab armed groups of targeting returnees while Damascus-aligned forces allegedly stood by. The US envoy to the UN told Rudaw he would follow the case.
  4. University mergers and curriculum changes spark controversy Governance and the new state · People: leaning negative · Outlets: mostly neutral · 1% of the discussion
    Syria's Higher Education Ministry ordered merging Rojava University (Qamishli) and Kobani University into al-Furat and Aleppo universities, alongside other mergers, decisions widely covered on 27-28 October. Kurdish students and outlets say this threatens Kurdish-language education, while separate reports allege curriculum edits removing women's images and historical content.
  5. SalamAir launches first Oman-Damascus flights Economy and services · Outlets: leaning positive · 1% of the discussion
    Oman's SalamAir began operating flights between Muscat and Damascus on 28 October, with two flights weekly, following a technical inspection of Damascus airport in late September. Syria's civil aviation authority head confirmed the agreement with the airline.
  6. Assassinations and unresolved violence in Deir ez-Zor and Hama countryside Security and violence · Outlets: leaning negative · 1% of the discussion
    Separate violent incidents were reported in eastern and central Syria: an interior ministry member was shot dead by unknown gunmen in Deir ez-Zor, an armed robbery attempt in Hama countryside left one man seriously wounded, a drive-by killing near Qara in rural Damascus, and an old blood-feud killing in a village near Qamishli.
  7. Transitional justice court sentences former security officer, holds new sessions Transitional justice · Outlets: mostly neutral · 0% of the discussion
    Syria's Fourth Criminal Court, specialized in transitional justice, held 10 sessions and sentenced former Political Security volunteer Mohammed Islam Arnaouti to 15 years for unlawful detention under Article 357. State and independent outlets covered the ruling on 27-28 October.
  8. Landmine explosion kills three civilians in Hasakah countryside Security and violence · Outlets: leaning negative · 0% of the discussion
    A landmine left over from war exploded in Umm Zar village, Shaddadi area, southern Hasakah countryside, on 27 October, killing three people and injuring two children. North Press and Enab Baladi reported the incident, highlighting the ongoing danger of war remnants in the north-east.
  9. Displaced families return from Jarabulus camps under 'Syria Without Camps' plan Refugees and return · Outlets: leaning positive · 0% of the discussion
    A convoy of 111 displaced families left Jarabulus's Bunyan 2 and Yousef Beik camps on 27 October, heading to original areas in Aleppo, Idlib, Hama, Homs and Deir ez-Zor provinces. Aleppo's social affairs directorate said the move implements Decree 59 and the 'Syria Without Camps' vision.
  10. Israeli incursions and detentions continue in Quneitra Israel and the south · Outlets: leaning negative · 0% of the discussion
    Israeli occupation forces conducted a ground incursion with military vehicles into the village of al-Mu'allaqa in Quneitra countryside, searching homes and detaining at least one person, while separately Israel arrested three young men in rural Quneitra. Levant24's tracker also logged continued Israeli strikes, checkpoints and property destruction across Syria.

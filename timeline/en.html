September 2026

23 September 2026 · Governance · International

Al-Sharaa addresses the UN General Assembly again

Speaking to the 81st session, al-Sharaa described a new phase of recovery, cited the return of about 3.5 million refugees and displaced people, and called on Israel to withdraw from Syrian territory.

Sources: UN News, Al Jazeera

August 2026

24 August 2026 · Governance · International · Key event

Syria removed from the US list of state sponsors of terrorism

The United States rescinded the designation Syria had carried since 1979, after a 45-day notice to Congress, and delisted Hayat Tahrir al-Sham as a terrorist entity.

Sources: Khaleej Times, Cleary Gottlieb

24 August 2026 · Security · South

Syria and Israel hold US-mediated talks in Jordan

Officials discussed ways to halt Israeli strikes and incursions and to revive negotiations on a security agreement, which had stalled when the war on Iran began; Israeli incursions in Quneitra continued.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Khaleej Times

11 August 2026 · Governance · Nationwide · Key event

Assad sentenced to death in absentia

A Damascus court convicted Bashar al-Assad in absentia of killings, torture and arbitrary arrest and sentenced him to death, along with Maher al-Assad; Atef Najib, tried in person, received the same sentence. Assad remains in Russia.

Sources: Khaleej Times, Pajhwok (Reuters)

July 2026

12 July 2026 · Governance · Nationwide · Key event

New People's Assembly holds its first session

The assembly elected Abdul Hamid al-Awak as speaker with 99 votes. It is tasked with drafting a permanent constitution; Suwayda's seats remained vacant.

Sources: AP (PBS), The New Arab

May 2026

11 May 2026 · Economy · International

EU restores full trade ties

The EU Council ended the 2011 partial suspension of the EU-Syria Cooperation Agreement, restoring its full application.

Sources: Al Jazeera, WAM

10 May 2026 · Governance · Nationwide

First government reshuffle

Al-Sharaa made the first changes to the government since Assad's fall, replacing the presidency's secretary-general, several ministers and the governors of Homs, Quneitra and Deir ez-Zor, amid protests over economic conditions.

Sources: Al Jazeera, The New Arab

April 2026

26 April 2026 · Governance · Nationwide

Trial of Atef Najib opens

The Damascus criminal court opened the trial of Atef Najib, former head of Political Security in Daraa, and referred Bashar al-Assad and other former officials for trial in absentia.

Sources: Enab Baladi, Al Jazeera

16 April 2026 · Security · Nationwide · Key event

US hands over its last bases

Syria took control of all the major bases where US forces had been deployed, ending a US military presence of about a decade; US Central Command said it would keep supporting counter-terrorism operations.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Al-Monitor

March 2026

19 March 2026 · Security · Suwayda

Renewed fighting in Suwayda and Israeli strikes

Clashes involving government forces, Bedouin tribes and Druze factions resumed in Suwayda. Israel struck Syrian army positions in the south, saying it was responding to attacks on Druze civilians; the government said it was conducting a security operation.

Sources: Al Jazeera, North Press

February 2026

28 February 2026 · Security · International

War on Iran reaches Syrian skies

The United States and Israel launched strikes on Iran. Iranian missiles aimed at Israel fell in southern Syria, causing deaths and injuries; a tentative ceasefire was announced about 40 days later.

Sources: Airwars, Enab Baladi

22 February 2026 · Security · North-east

Al-Hol camp closed

Syrian authorities closed al-Hol, which had held families linked to suspected Islamic State fighters, after moving its remaining residents. Aid groups criticised an abrupt closure, and Human Rights Watch said the fate of about 8,500 people from the north-east's camps was unknown.

Sources: Al-Monitor, MSF

11 February 2026 · Security · East

US forces leave al-Tanf

US Central Command completed its withdrawal from the al-Tanf garrison near the Jordanian and Iraqi borders, and Syrian government forces took control of the base.

Sources: Al Arabiya, North Press

January 2026

30 January 2026 · Governance · North-east · Key event

Comprehensive agreement between Damascus and the SDF

A US-brokered agreement set out a comprehensive ceasefire, the phased integration of the SDF and the north-east's civil institutions into the state, the deployment of Interior Ministry forces into Hasakah and Qamishli, and guarantees of Kurdish civil and educational rights.

Sources: AP (Stars and Stripes), Enab Baladi

18 January 2026 · Security · North-east · Key event

Government offensive in the north-east and ceasefire

After an offensive launched on 13 January, government forces took Tabqa and its dam. A ceasefire on 18 January provided for the SDF to withdraw from areas west of the Euphrates and hand Raqqa and Deir ez-Zor to the government.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Euronews

6 January 2026 · Security · Aleppo

Renewed fighting in Aleppo

Government forces and the SDF fought again in Sheikh Maqsoud and Ashrafiyeh. A ceasefire followed, with SDF fighters in the two districts to be moved to the north-east.

Sources: Al Jazeera, AP (Stars and Stripes)

1 January 2026 · Economy · Nationwide

New Syrian pound banknotes

The central bank began replacing banknotes with a new currency that removes two zeros, exchanging 100 old pounds for one new pound, with old notes circulating alongside for a transition period.

Sources: Kuwait Times, Khaleej Times

December 2025

19 December 2025 · Economy · International · Key event

US repeals the Caesar Act

President Trump signed the 2026 defence bill, which repeals the Caesar Act sanctions (reports give 18 or 19 December). The Senate had passed it 77 to 20; the law requires regular reports on Syria's conduct.

Sources: Enab Baladi, The National

13 December 2025 · Security · Central Syria

Attack on US forces near Palmyra

A gunman killed two US soldiers and a US civilian interpreter near Palmyra. US officials said the Islamic State group was probably behind it; local officials told Reuters the attacker was a member of the Syrian security forces.

Sources: TimesLIVE (Reuters), The War Zone

November 2025

10 November 2025 · Governance · International · Key event

Al-Sharaa at the White House; Syria joins the coalition against IS

Al-Sharaa became the first Syrian president to visit the White House. Syria announced it would join the Global Coalition to Defeat ISIS, and Caesar Act sanctions were suspended for another six months.

Sources: Axios, Al-Monitor

6 November 2025 · Governance · International

UN Security Council lifts sanctions on al-Sharaa

The Council voted 14 to 0, with China abstaining, to remove al-Sharaa and Interior Minister Anas Khattab from its ISIL and al-Qaeda sanctions list.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Anadolu Agency

October 2025

15 October 2025 · Governance · International

Al-Sharaa meets Putin in Moscow

On his first official visit to Russia, al-Sharaa told President Putin that Syria would honour past agreements with Moscow, a pledge read as securing Russia's two main bases.

Sources: Al-Monitor, Euronews

6 October 2025 · Security · Aleppo

Clashes in Aleppo's Sheikh Maqsoud and Ashrafiyeh

Fighting broke out between government forces and the SDF in the two Kurdish-majority neighbourhoods of Aleppo, ending with a ceasefire the next day.

Sources: Enab Baladi, Al Arabiya

5 October 2025 · Governance · Nationwide

Indirect elections to the People's Assembly

About 6,000 members of electoral colleges chose 119 of the 140 elected seats in a 210-seat assembly; the president appoints a third of the seats. Voting was not held in Suwayda, Raqqa and Hasakah.

Sources: Inter-Parliamentary Union, International IDEA

September 2025

24 September 2025 · Governance · International

First address by a Syrian president to the UN General Assembly since 1967

Al-Sharaa told the General Assembly that Syria was reclaiming its place among nations, vowed to prosecute those responsible for violence and criticised Israeli military action.

Sources: AP, The New Arab

July 2025

24 July 2025 · Economy · International

Syrian-Saudi Investment Forum

Syria and Saudi Arabia signed 47 investment agreements and memoranda worth about $6.4 billion at a forum in Damascus, covering real estate, infrastructure, telecommunications and energy.

Sources: bne IntelliNews, Gulf News

13 July 2025 · Security · Suwayda · Key event

Violence in Suwayda

Clashes between Druze factions and Bedouin tribes drew in government forces, and Israel struck government forces and the Defence Ministry in Damascus. A ceasefire took hold around 19 July. Death tolls range from 260 (the Health Ministry's hospital count) to about 1,490 (a war monitor).

Sources: Steptoe, Rudaw

June 2025

30 June 2025 · Economy · International

US executive order ends the Syria sanctions program

Executive Order 14312 ended the US Syria sanctions program from 1 July, while keeping sanctions on Assad and his associates, captagon traffickers and terrorist groups. The Caesar Act remained law.

Sources: White House (via GlobalSecurity), Holland & Knight

22 June 2025 · Security · Damascus

Suicide bombing at Mar Elias church

A suicide attack during Mass at the Greek Orthodox Mar Elias church in Damascus killed at least 22 people. The interior ministry said the attacker belonged to the Islamic State group.

Sources: Arab News, Vatican News

May 2025

20 May 2025 · Economy · International

EU decides to lift economic sanctions

EU foreign ministers decided to lift economic sanctions on Syria; the legal acts followed on 28 May. Measures against people linked to the Assad government and on security grounds stayed in place.

Sources: EU (EEAS), European Commission

13 May 2025 · Economy · International · Key event

Trump announces the lifting of US sanctions

In Riyadh, President Trump said the United States would lift its sanctions on Syria, and met al-Sharaa the next day, the first meeting between US and Syrian presidents since 2000.

Sources: Axios, NPR

2 May 2025 · Security · Damascus

Jaramana and Sahnaya clashes; Israeli strike near the presidential palace

Days of fighting between Druze and Sunni gunmen in Jaramana and Sahnaya, set off by a recording insulting the Prophet, killed more than 100 people according to a war monitor. Israel then struck near the presidential palace in Damascus, saying it was protecting the Druze; Syria called it a dangerous escalation.

Sources: Gulf News, NBC News

March 2025

29 March 2025 · Governance · Nationwide

Transitional government of 23 ministers

Al-Sharaa announced a 23-member cabinet with no prime minister, including Alawite, Druze and Christian ministers. The autonomous administration in the north-east said it did not reflect the country's diversity.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Khaleej Times

13 March 2025 · Governance · Nationwide · Key event

Constitutional Declaration signed

The declaration set a five-year transitional period, made Islamic jurisprudence the main source of legislation and required the president to be Muslim. Supporters pointed to its rights provisions; critics said it concentrated power in the presidency.

Sources: Anadolu Agency, Al-Monitor

10 March 2025 · Governance · North-east

Agreement to integrate the SDF into the state

Ahmed al-Sharaa and SDF commander Mazloum Abdi signed a short framework agreement to merge the civil and military institutions of the north-east into the state by the end of the year, with committees to work out the details.

Sources: Enab Baladi, Enab Baladi

6 March 2025 · Security · Coast · Key event

Mass killings on the coast

Attacks on security forces by gunmen loyal to the former government were followed by days of killings in the coastal region, mostly of Alawite civilians. A government inquiry counted more than 1,400 dead and the UN Commission of Inquiry about 1,400, most of them civilians; a war monitor put the toll above 1,700. The UN commission said abuses by government forces and allied groups may amount to war crimes.

Sources: AP, Al-Monitor

February 2025

25 February 2025 · Governance · Nationwide

National Dialogue Conference

Hundreds of participants met in Damascus on 24 and 25 February. The final statement stressed the country's unity and called for a committee to draft a new constitution; some political and opposition figures criticised the preparation and representation.

Sources: Al-Monitor, Vatican News

January 2025

29 January 2025 · Governance · Nationwide · Key event

Ahmed al-Sharaa named transitional president

At a meeting of armed factions in Damascus, Ahmed al-Sharaa was named president for the transitional period. The 2012 constitution was suspended, and parliament, the former army and security agencies, the Baath party and the armed factions, including Hayat Tahrir al-Sham, were declared dissolved.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Al Jazeera

December 2024

10 December 2024 · Governance · Nationwide

Caretaker government under Mohammed al-Bashir

Mohammed al-Bashir, who had headed the Salvation Government in Idlib, was named caretaker prime minister with a mandate until 1 March 2025.

Sources: Al Jazeera, L'Orient-Le Jour

9 December 2024 · Security · South · Key event

Israeli forces enter the buffer zone and Mount Hermon

Israeli troops crossed into the UN-patrolled buffer zone and onto the Syrian side of Mount Hermon, while Israel carried out hundreds of strikes on Syrian military assets. Critics said the move violated the 1974 disengagement agreement; Israel said it was acting in self-defence.

Sources: L'Orient-Le Jour, Al-Monitor

8 December 2024 · Governance · Nationwide · Key event

The Assad government falls

After an offensive led by Hayat Tahrir al-Sham that began on 27 November, opposition forces entered Damascus. Bashar al-Assad left for Moscow, where Russian state media said he was granted asylum, ending more than five decades of Assad family rule.

Sources: AP, AP (Anchorage Daily News)

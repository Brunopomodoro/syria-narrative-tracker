1. The day at a glance

Frustrated Syrians continued to debate economic hardship, with the lira nearing 15,000 to the dollar and a modest diesel price cut that most commenters called cosmetic. Diplomatic meetings (Syria-Lebanon, Syria-Jordan) and infrastructure projects (Aleppo airport, power plant) got official attention, while Israeli incursions in the south and north-east judicial integration disputes continued. A sermon criticizing economic conditions sparked wide, polarized public reaction.

  1. Car Bomb Kills Man and Girl in Jaramana Security and violence · People: strongly negative · Outlets: leaning negative · 21% of the discussion
    A car bomb exploded in Jaramana, a Damascus suburb with a large Druze and minority population, on November 14-15, killing a man and a child and wounding others. Syrian Internal Security Forces said they are investigating; reports identified the dead man as the son of a Shia cleric of Pakistani origin.
  2. Diesel Price Cut to 150 Lira Meets Public Skepticism Economy and services · People: strongly negative · Outlets: mostly neutral · 21% of the discussion
    Syria's pricing committee cut diesel from 175 to 150 lira per liter starting November 15, a 14% reduction after September's 40% hike, while keeping gasoline and gas prices unchanged. Officials framed it as easing winter heating burdens. Many commenters called the cut too small given wages and overall price levels.

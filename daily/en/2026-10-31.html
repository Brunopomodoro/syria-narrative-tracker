    Syria's Foreign Minister Asaad al-Shaibani spoke at the 22nd Manama Dialogue, saying Syria faced over 1,000 Israeli attacks in under two years and reaffirming the Golan as Syrian land. He said Syria is weighing joining the Mecca defense pact and stressed cooperation with Jordan, Iraq and Gulf states. State and independent outlets gave this extensive, largely positive coverage.
